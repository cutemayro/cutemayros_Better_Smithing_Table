## How To Upgrade Your Tools
**Currently The Datapack Only Offers Upgrading Of Swords, Tools And Armor**

To Upgrade A Tool You Need 2 Of The Higher Progression Tier Materials And The Tool It Self

By Putting The Material In The Template And Material Slot With The Tool In The Middle You Can Upgrade Your Tool
